Your study

A simulated study tested 90 participants. Each used a laptop, tablet or pen and paper, and completed tests on two matched topics: one reviewed and one not reviewed.

Review improved scores in all three media. Its average benefit was:

Laptop5.18points
Tablet15.58points
Paper15.90points

Tablet and paper did not differ significantly. Tablet notes were handwritten with a stylus. The study measured test scores, but not note quality or depth of processing.

Research context: Mueller and Oppenheimer (2014) reported an interaction involving note medium and review. Morehead and colleagues (2019) found less consistent differences between media.

These are fabricated teaching data. The pattern illustrates an interaction.

Start with the research question, then summarise and interpret the findings. Evaluate them against the literature before choosing a limitation, a future study and a closing line. Your answers become a presentation slide with your percentage score.
