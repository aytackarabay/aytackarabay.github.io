How to read these figures

Large markers and bar heights show means. Boxplot centre lines show medians, boxes show the middle 50% of observations, and whiskers extend to observations within 1.5 times the interquartile range. Small dots show observations. Rainclouds add a smoothed estimate of the distribution.

Confidence intervals describe the mean in the displayed group. They are pointwise 95% t intervals. Overlap between these intervals is not a significance test, especially for paired observations. The statistical results use the original mixed ANOVA and its appropriate participant error terms.

In the study medium view, each observation is a participant's average across the two review conditions. In the review benefit view, each observation is a participant's score with review minus their score without review.

Participant connections are available only for the interaction and review views, with individual observations visible. Rainclouds require dots and boxplots, so their controls are fixed. Standard bars do not support the boxplot or paired connection layers in this explorer.

Stars indicate * p < .05, ** p < .01 and *** p < .001. Nonsignificant comparisons receive no symbol. Pairwise comparisons use Holm adjustment within the families stated beneath the figure. Nonsignificance does not establish equivalence.

Teaching simulation. Laptop notes are typed; tablet notes are handwritten with a stylus; paper notes are handwritten with a pen. Each participant contributes scores for two distinct matched topics, one reviewed and one not reviewed.
